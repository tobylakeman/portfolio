Octobia Multimedia Design · Reel 001

Brand, print and motion, run as one.

A multidisciplinary design and production studio: identity, print, motion, and photography, built so a logo, a catalogue, and a launch film all read like they came from the same place.

00 · The Studio

One studio. Every format it needs.

Octobia was built on a simple bet: most brands don’t have a content problem, they have a fragmentation problem. A logo from one studio, a catalogue from another, a launch film from a third rarely feels like the same company.

We build the identity, the print collateral, and the motion work in one place: brand systems, packaging, and photography sitting on the same shelf as motion graphics, editing, and sound design, run out of one studio, one voice.
