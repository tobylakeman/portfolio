In motion.

A view into 25 years across motion graphics, video editing, broadcast and brand work.

MOTION DESIGN SHOWREELA curated mix of projects from across my career, motion design, video editing and sound design woven together into a single reel.

PAUL BENNET AIRSHOWS - WARBIRDS OVER SCONEWhile working with PBA, I shot and designed content for every facet of the airshow. I hired a Sony FX6, then filmed, edited and delivered content across the 3-day event.

KINGS OF NEONAn overview of the content I created during my 16-month period with Kings of Neon, brand videos, social media (paid ads and organic posts), how-to films and explainer videos.

OMNIA PLANT GLIDERWhile working with Omnia Wheel, I created content for one of their products, the Omnia Plant Glider. The role included filming, editing and creating motion graphics for paid ads, organic content and the product's website.

BENGALLA - CORPORATE VIDEOMade while working for Out of the Square Media. I was the sole video editor, motion designer and sound designer on this large-scale project. Three on-the-ground camera operators, two UAV pilots and the Creative Director I worked alongside daily shot the footage; I coordinated and organised the whole job from start to finish.

PENN - START YOUR OWN BATTLE FISHING SERIERS PENN is one of the most recognised names in fishing. Being chosen to design the intro, outro and titles for the Start Your Own Battle series was a direct result of the motion work I had done previously for the client. Energetic, polished, and built to give the series a visual identity it could own from the first frame to the last.

About
Toby.

"FREEDOM LIES IN BEING BOLD."

Hello,

I'm a highly skilled motion designer, video editor, videographer, graphic designer, and client liaison with 25 years of experience across the design, marketing, and video industries. I'm passionate about design, storytelling, and creating content that connects with audiences and delivers meaningful results.

I bring a proactive, creative, and solutions-focused approach to every project, while genuinely valuing collaboration and the ideas of others. I believe the best outcomes come from asking thoughtful questions, staying curious, and continually looking for better ways to work.

For me, learning is an ongoing process of asking:

"What am I doing? Why am I doing it this way? Is there a better approach? How can I continue to grow? And how can I create the best possible experience for clients while staying true to my values and achieving strong results?"

This mindset has helped me build lasting relationships, solve problems creatively, and deliver work that is both effective and engaging.

I'd love the opportunity to bring my experience, creativity, and enthusiasm to your team.
